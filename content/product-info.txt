PLANT PARENTHOOD — PRODUCT CATALOG
BUSINESS / PRODUCT OVERVIEW
Plant Parenthood sells houseplants in upcycled glass bottle planters, along with Plant Parenthood merchandise.
The planters are made from repurposed bottles. Each bottle is carefully cut, smoothed, and polished to create a safe, decorative planter with drainage.
