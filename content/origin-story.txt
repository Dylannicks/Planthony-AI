Plant Parenthood began operating in Fall 2022, under a bed in the Eringhaus Dormitories at UNC Chapel Hill. The early focus was on planting and growing plants. During 2023, the business began honing its pop-up sale model, launched its Instagram, and gained recognition from The Daily Tar Heel. One of its campus sales sold out, becoming its biggest campus sale at the time. The team also developed its products around the needs of college students, focusing on hardy, low-maintenance indoor plants suited to dorms and other small spaces, paired with unique and sustainably sourced or reused planters.
In 2024, Plant Parenthood placed 2nd in the inaugural Joan and Chester Luby Pitch Competition and subsequently participated in Seed Money, a televised student venture pitch competition. The company also secured paid opportunities with UNC Kenan-Flagler Business School for its Earth Day initiative and UNC for a retirement party. The team later achieved its first autonomous sale, demonstrating that its sales and fulfillment systems could operate without direct owner involvement.
In 2025, Plant Parenthood expanded its social media marketing through TikTok and Instagram and purchased a greenhouse to increase inventory for the upcoming school year. The company has also developed a sustainability focus, including repurposing bottles and other materials into planters; the venture reported diverting more than 450 bottles from landfills.
In 2026, the founder received the Gerald Unks Fellowship, a personal award whose story was connected in part to the journey of building Plant Parenthood. Plant Parenthood also received a $7,500 award supporting an international exploration of plant culture during Summer 2026.
