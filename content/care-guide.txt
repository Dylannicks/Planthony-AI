Plant Parenthood’s Easy-Care Plant Guide
A simple guide to keeping your plants thriving!
________________


1. Pothos (Epipremnum aureum)
Light: Thrives in low to bright indirect light. Avoid direct sunlight, which can scorch leaves.
Water: Allow soil to dry out between waterings. Water when top 1-2 inches of soil are dry.
Humidity: Prefers moderate to high humidity but tolerates normal room conditions.
Soil: Well-draining potting mix.
Fertilizer: Feed monthly with diluted liquid fertilizer in spring and summer.
Common Issues & Solutions:
* Yellow Leaves → Overwatering. Let soil dry before watering again. Ensure pot has drainage.
* Brown, Crispy Leaves → Underwatering or low humidity. Increase watering frequency and mist occasionally.
* Leggy Growth → Not enough light. Move to a brighter spot. Trim back stems to encourage bushier growth.

2. Spider Plant (Chlorophytum comosum)
Light: Prefers bright, indirect light but tolerates low light.
Water: Water when the top inch of soil is dry. Avoid soggy soil.
Humidity: Adaptable, but enjoys occasional misting.
Soil: Well-draining potting mix.
Fertilizer: Every 2-4 weeks in growing seasons (spring & summer).
Common Issues & Solutions:
* Brown Leaf Tips → Fluoride or chlorine in tap water. Use filtered or distilled water.
* Wilting Leaves → Overwatering or root rot. Allow soil to dry out and ensure proper drainage.
* Lack of “Spiderettes” (Baby Plants) → Needs more light or fertilizer. Move to a brighter spot and feed monthly.

3. Christmas Cactus (Schlumbergera spp.)
Light: Bright, indirect light. Avoid prolonged direct sun.
Water: Water when top 1-2 inches of soil are dry. Keep soil slightly moist during blooming.
Humidity: Prefers high humidity; mist or use a pebble tray.
Soil: Well-draining cactus or succulent mix.
Fertilizer: Use a balanced fertilizer every month in spring and summer. Reduce feeding in fall and winter.
Common Issues & Solutions:
* Shriveled or Wrinkled Leaves → Underwatering. Increase watering but avoid waterlogging.
* Dropping Buds → Sudden temperature changes or too much movement. Keep in a stable environment.
* Red/Purple-Tinted Leaves → Too much direct sunlight. Move to filtered light.

4. Philodendron (Philodendron spp.)
Light: Thrives in medium to bright indirect light. Avoid prolonged direct sun.
Water: Water when the top inch of soil is dry.
Humidity: Prefers moderate to high humidity but tolerates normal indoor levels.
Soil: Well-draining potting mix.
Fertilizer: Feed monthly during growing season with a diluted liquid fertilizer.
Common Issues & Solutions:
* Yellow Leaves → Overwatering. Let soil dry out before watering again.
* Droopy Leaves → Can indicate both overwatering and underwatering. Check soil moisture before adjusting.
* Leggy Growth → Needs more light. Move closer to a bright window.
________________


5. Jade Plant (Crassula ovata)
Light: Prefers bright, indirect light. Some direct sun is beneficial.
Water: Water deeply but infrequently. Allow soil to dry out completely between waterings.
Humidity: Low humidity preferred.
Soil: Well-draining succulent or cactus mix.
Fertilizer: Fertilize every 2-3 months in growing season.
Common Issues & Solutions:
* Wrinkled or Shriveling Leaves → Underwatering. Water thoroughly but let dry before next watering.
* Soft, Mushy Leaves → Overwatering. Check for root rot and reduce watering.
* Leggy Growth → Insufficient light. Move to a sunnier location.
________________


6. Monstera (Monstera deliciosa)
Light: Bright, indirect light. Can tolerate low light but grows slower.
Water: Water when the top 2 inches of soil are dry.
Humidity: Prefers high humidity. Mist occasionally.
Soil: Well-draining potting mix with added perlite.
Fertilizer: Every 2 weeks in spring/summer; reduce in winter.
Common Issues & Solutions:
* Yellow Leaves → Overwatering. Ensure proper drainage.
* Brown Leaf Edges → Low humidity. Increase misting or use a humidifier.
* Small or No Fenestrations (Leaf Holes) → Not enough light. Move to a brighter spot.

7. Pencil Cactus (Euphorbia tirucalli)
Light: Needs bright, direct sunlight. Best near a sunny window.
Water: Water sparingly. Allow soil to dry out completely before watering.
Humidity: Low humidity preferred.
Soil: Well-draining succulent or cactus mix.
Fertilizer: Every 2 months in spring/summer.
Common Issues & Solutions:
* Drooping or Wrinkled Stems → Underwatering. Give a deep soak and let dry completely.
